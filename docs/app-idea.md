# Line Dance Platform - App Idea

## Overview

The Line Dance Platform is a centralized social and discovery platform designed specifically for the line dancing community. The goal of the app is to combine several fragmented tools and experiences into one organized platform where dancers can discover dances, learn choreography, find events, interact with the community, and stay updated on trending songs and dances.

Currently, dancers often rely on multiple separate platforms such as:
- YouTube for tutorials
- TikTok for short dance clips
- GroupMe or Facebook groups for communication
- Spotify or Apple Music for songs
- Word of mouth for learning new dances and events

This creates a fragmented experience where discovering dances, learning choreography, and staying involved in the community can become difficult and unorganized.

The purpose of this app is to centralize these experiences into one platform designed specifically for dancers.

---

# Main Goals

## Goal 1: Dance Discovery
Allow users to discover:
- new dances
- trending dances
- recommended dances
- popular songs used in dancing
- choreography creators

---

## Goal 2: Learning Platform
Provide easy access to:
- tutorial videos
- dance walkthroughs
- difficulty levels
- dance descriptions
- song information

The app may initially use external links from platforms such as YouTube and TikTok instead of hosting videos directly.

---

## Goal 3: Community Features
Create a social space for dancers through:
- profiles
- comments
- likes
- saved dances
- group/event discussions
- dance requests

---

## Goal 4: Event Discovery
Allow users to:
- find dance events
- view event details
- see event locations
- share upcoming events
- connect with local dance communities

---

## Goal 5: Analytics and Trends
Track and analyze:
- trending dances
- popular songs
- engagement activity
- dance popularity over time
- user interaction trends

This feature may later connect to machine learning and recommendation systems.

---
